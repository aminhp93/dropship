Methodology & limitations

This report started as 20 car‑accessory products researched live on Aug 9, 2026 (an initial 5‑product pass, then a 15‑product expansion parallelized across five background research passes), then was trimmed down to just the top 10 by opportunity score. All passes followed the same checklist — correct English keywords, demand reviewed at both 5‑year and 12‑month Trends windows plus interest‑by‑region and related/rising queries, Google Shopping, TikTok, and Meta Ads Library, all opened live. Honest gaps, flagged rather than papered over:

  • Google Keyword Planner requires an authenticated Google Ads account, which wasn't available in this session. Search‑volume figures throughout are third‑party estimates or Trends‑relative‑index readings, explicitly labeled as estimates rather than Planner‑grade numbers.
  • Meta Audience Insights (the standalone tool) was discontinued by Meta in 2024. The Meta Ads Library was used as the live substitute instead — but its keyword match is fuzzy and varies by product. Car Trash Cans and Roadside Emergency Kits both returned a real active‑ad count where the ads actually served on manual review were unrelated to the product. That's reported as a real finding — thin dedicated Meta ad spend in those niches — not hidden as a tooling failure.
  • Two of the five parallel research passes that produced the original 20‑product set (the Interior LED Strips group, and the Steering Wheel Covers/Sun Shades group) noted the shared browser environment occasionally showed stray tabs or reloaded queries from other concurrent passes. Each flagged and re‑verified its own data against the correct live query before recording it — noted here for full transparency rather than left unsaid.
  • Screenshots were captured and reviewed live for every chart, listing, and search shown in this pass — but the browser tool has no way to export them as image files, so they can't be embedded in this document. Each "Live source views" block links to the exact same query/filters used, so you can reopen the identical live view at any time.
